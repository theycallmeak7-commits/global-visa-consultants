Our specialty

Business migration

For founders, owners and multinational groups who need to move themselves, their staff or their company across a border. We handle the corporate structure, the visa route and the residence application as one coordinated file.

  • 01

    USA E-2 Investor Visa

    For entrepreneurs investing in a business in the United States. Treaty investors, their spouse and children under 21 receive work-authorised status tied to that business. No labour certification is required, but the business must be genuine and actively operating for the status to be maintained.

    Discuss this route
  • 02

    USA L-1 Visa

    For employees of a multinational group transferring to its United States parent, subsidiary or affiliate. L-1A covers managers and executives; L-1B covers members with specialised organisational or technical expertise. It is a dual-intent route, so it can lead to permanent residence.

    Discuss this route
  • 03

    UK Expansion Worker Visa

    A route for people already in the United Kingdom on a Student or other visa who want to switch into skilled employment without needing a job offer at the point of switch, then extend in the Skilled Worker category.

    Discuss this route
  • 04

    Portugal D2 Visa

    For entrepreneurs establishing or investing in a Portuguese company, or joining an existing one as a director or administrator. Self-employment is permitted, family members can accompany you, and the visa leads to a residence permit.

    Discuss this route
  • 05

    UAE Business Formation

    Setting up a company in the Emirates through a mainland or free-zone structure. We cover the trade licence, the ownership and profit structure, and the residency visas for the investor and employees.

    Discuss this route
  • 06

    Canada C-11 Start-Up Visa

    Permanent residence for entrepreneurs who secure a letter of support from a designated organisation, hold a qualifying ownership position, meet the language and funds requirements, and intend to operate a business in Canada.

    Discuss this route

Skilled immigration

For professionals and skilled workers

Employer-sponsored, self-petition and points-tested routes for people whose qualification, experience or craft is the basis of their application.

  • 01

    EB-2 NIW

    The National Interest Waiver lets professionals with an advanced degree or exceptional ability petition for permanent residence themselves, with no employer sponsorship and no labour certification.

    Discuss this route
  • 02

    UK Global Talent Visa

    A five-year, sponsor-free visa for leaders and potential leaders in their field, backed by an endorsement from a recognised body, with a clear route to settlement.

    Discuss this route
  • 03

    Australia National Innovation Visa

    The subclass 494 pathway, with Global Talent, Global Graduate, Specialist and Work in Innovation streams, for people who contribute to Australia through their expertise. It can lead to permanent residence.

    Discuss this route
  • 04

    Canada FSW & PNP Programs

    The Federal Skilled Worker Program runs through Express Entry points, while the Provincial Nominee Programs add a provincial nomination to widen selection for candidates with a strong profile.

    Discuss this route
  • 05

    Australia Subclass 189, 190 & 491

    Subclasses 189 and 190 are points-tested routes to permanent residence, with 190 adding a state nomination. Subclass 491 is a three-year provisional visa for regional areas, with a pathway to 191 permanence.

    Discuss this route
  • 06

    Germany Chancenkarte

    Germany's Opportunity Card is a points-based entry route requiring six or more points, allowing part-time work of up to 20 hours a week for up to one year, extendable to two.

    Discuss this route

Remote work

Digital nomad visas

A digital nomad visa generally allows a person to live temporarily in a country while working remotely for an employer, business, or clients outside that country. The biggest differences are income, whether employment is required, duration, family rights, and whether the route can lead to permanent residence.

Major European digital nomad options

  • Spain

    For non-EU nationals working remotely for a foreign employer or clients, renewable for as long as the income requirement is met.

  • Portugal

    One of the most established routes to EU residence for remote workers, renewable annually and convertible to a residence permit.

  • Greece

    A one-year residence permit for remote workers earning above a set monthly threshold, extendable where the conditions continue to be met.

  • Malta

    A one-year nomad residence permit that can be extended to three years and, in time, converted into permanent residence.

  • Cyprus

    A one-year temporary residence permit for third-country remote workers, renewable for a longer stay in the same capacity.

  • Italy

    A one-year digital nomad and remote worker visa for people living abroad and working remotely, renewable for longer stays.

Residence routes

Residency without employment

  • 01

    Portugal D7 Visa

    A residence visa for remote workers, retirees and pensioners with regular passive or remote income generated outside Portugal. You do not need to hold a Portuguese job, and the route can lead to a residence permit and, after the required years, to Portuguese citizenship.

    Discuss this route
  • 02

    Spain Non-Lucrative Visa

    For people who can support themselves without working in Spain. Applicants need sufficient financial means, valid private health insurance and a clean criminal record. The visa is granted for one year, is renewable, and family members can live with you. Holders may also work limited hours for an employer outside Spain with prior authorisation.

    Discuss this route
